Our story

About Schwick’s Picks

Schwick’s Picks is a small, family-run floral studio. What started as arranging flowers for friends and family grew into a little business built around one idea: beautiful arrangements shouldn’t have to be fussy or fleeting.

Why faux flowers

Our specialty is faux (artificial) arrangements made with premium materials — real-touch petals, lifelike greenery, natural-looking stems. Done well, they’re hard to tell from fresh, and they keep their color and shape for years. No water, no wilting, no waste.

We still love fresh flowers, and we design with them for the occasions that call for it. And we take on wreaths, custom commissions, and event décor — if you can picture it, we’d like to hear about it.
