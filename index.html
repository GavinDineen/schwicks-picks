Welcome

A little floral studio with a lasting touch

Schwick’s Picks is a small, family-run floral business specializing in faux (artificial) flower arrangements that look and feel real — no wilting, no water, just lasting beauty. We also design with fresh flowers and create wreaths, centerpieces, and custom pieces for special occasions.
