Services

From lasting faux arrangements to fresh seasonal blooms and one-of-a-kind custom pieces — here’s what Schwick’s Picks can create for you.

Our specialty

Faux Flower Arrangements

Our main craft. We use premium artificial florals — real-touch petals, natural stems, quality greenery — arranged for realism and balance. They never wilt, need no water, and hold up for years, which makes them ideal for mantels, dining tables, entryways, offices, and gifts for people who’d rather not fuss with upkeep.

  • Table centerpieces and mantel arrangements
  • Everyday vases and accent pieces
  • Seasonal and holiday arrangements
  • Low-maintenance gift arrangements
  • Wedding and event florals that travel and keep

Real / Fresh Flower Arrangements

When the occasion calls for fresh flowers, we design seasonal arrangements using what’s freshest and best. Great for birthdays, anniversaries, sympathy, hostess gifts, and celebrations.

  • Seasonal hand-tied bouquets
  • Vase arrangements for delivery or pickup
  • Sympathy and get-well arrangements
  • Special-occasion centerpieces
